This page defines the things the API works with, in one place. Each section links to the guide that goes deeper.

## Workspace

A **workspace** is one account's space in Yungle: its transfers, collections, contacts, webhooks and storage. It has one **owner**, whose plan decides what the workspace can do and whose storage its files use.

- **Members** are people the owner adds to the workspace. They work in it from the dashboard.
- **Guests** are people invited by email into a single collection. They see that collection and nothing else.

An **API key** belongs to one workspace and acts as its owner. A **scope** limits which resources the key may read or change. See [Authentication](/authentication).

## Transfer

A **transfer** is a set of files sent as one link that expires. It moves through these states:

1. **Draft.** You create the transfer and upload its files. Nothing is live and nobody is emailed. A draft that is never sent is deleted after 24 hours, unless one of its uploads has made progress in the last 6 hours.
2. **Sent.** You finalize it. The link at `url` starts working, and any **recipients** you named are emailed it. `finalizedAt` is set and `status` is `active`.
3. **Expired or revoked.** At `expiresAt` the link stops working and the files are deleted, and `status` becomes `expired`. Revoking does the same at once, and `status` becomes `removed`.

Every transfer expires. On the free plan a transfer lasts {freeDays} days and holds up to {freeTransfer}; a paid plan lets you choose a longer lifetime. You can also protect a transfer with a password or cap how many times it is downloaded. Address a transfer by its `id`; the `slug` in its link is a secret that lets anyone download.

Guide: [Send a transfer](/guides/send-a-transfer).

## Collection

A **collection** is a durable space for files, organized in **folders** (up to 10 levels deep), usually one per client or project. On a paid plan nothing in a collection expires. You share a collection through its secret link, or invite guests by email. Collections need a Leaf or Tree plan, with {leafQuota} or {treeQuota} of storage shared by all of a workspace's collections.

Guide: [Client collections](/guides/client-collections).

## Upload request

An **upload request** is a public page that lets anyone with its link upload files into one of your collections, without an account. You can add a password, an expiry and a checklist of what you expect. You can pause it and resume it, or close it for good. Each upload through it is a submission, and the request records who sent what.

Guide: [Receive files](/guides/receive-files).

## Contact

A **contact** is an entry in the workspace's address book: an email address, which is unique per workspace, with an optional name, company, phone number and type.

Guide: [Contacts](/guides/contacts).

## Files and scanning

Each file in a transfer or collection is uploaded with [tus](https://tus.io), a resumable protocol, or fetched by Yungle from a URL you give it. Once a file is uploaded, Yungle scans it for viruses. A file's `scanResult` is `null` until the scan has run and `clean` after. An infected file is destroyed: its key is deleted, so it can never be downloaded.

Each file also carries a `crc32` checksum, so you can check a download arrived intact.

Guides: [Upload large files](/guides/upload-large-files), [Download files](/guides/download-files).

## Encryption

Every file is always encrypted, each with its own key, which Yungle holds. Because Yungle holds the keys, it can scan files for viruses and show previews.

Two features go further and are end-to-end encrypted: the files are encrypted on your device and the key never reaches Yungle.

- **End-to-end encrypted transfers.** You opt in per transfer. The key travels in the `#` part of the link, so such a transfer cannot be emailed by Yungle or password-protected.
- **The vault.** A private collection encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase. The API cannot reach it.

See [What the API cannot do](/unsupported) for what this means for your integration.

## Plans and metering

Yungle has a free plan and two paid plans, Leaf and Tree. The free plan covers transfers, contacts and webhooks; collections and upload requests need a paid plan.

Uploads you make through the API are metered on their own, the same way on every plan: the first {apiFreeUsage} each month are free, then {apiPricePerGb} per GB from prepaid credit. Downloads are never metered. [Limits](/limits) lists the request and email limits; [Pricing](https://yungle.co/pricing) has the plans.
